Daily Outlook

Based on Friday, 18 September 2026 close · gold market closed for the weekend · written by the desk, gold only

Gold heads into the weekend near $4,380, having recovered from a one-and-a-half-month low below $4,250 earlier in the week and set to close out a three-week losing streak. The bounce came as Treasury yields pulled back from multi-year highs — the 10-year slipped to roughly 4.9% after briefly topping 5% — while falling oil prices eased inflation worries and helped offset the hawkish message of the Fed's first rate hike in three years.

The technical picture, however, is a recovery rather than a reversal. Price is still trading below the 200-day moving average, daily RSI sits just above neutral, and MACD remains in negative territory, which suggests upside attempts are fragile. The $4,450 area is the ceiling to watch, having capped rallies on September 8, 9 and 10, while the recent low below $4,250 marks the floor buyers need to defend.

Next week the main drivers are Treasury yields, the dollar's reaction to the Fed's tightening cycle, and oil-driven inflation expectations, with ADP employment on Tuesday, preliminary PMIs on Wednesday, jobless claims on Thursday and University of Michigan inflation expectations on Friday. Bias: neutral to cautiously constructive. A daily close above $4,450 would be the first real sign the correction is over, while a break back below $4,250 would reopen the downside and put the recent low back in play.
